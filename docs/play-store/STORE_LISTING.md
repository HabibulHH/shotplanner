### Full description

ShotKit is a free, offline shot list planner for filmmakers, videographers and wedding shooters. Plan every shot before the shoot, then run your shot list on set — no account, no signal, no subscription.

PLAN EVERY SHOT

Build your shot list scene by scene. Pick the shot size, camera angle, movement, lens and camera with a guided builder and animated framing previews. Every shot gets a framing picture, so your plan reads like a quick storyboard even before you add reference photos.

In a hurry? Type a description in the quick-add bar to drop a shot in seconds, then refine it later.

RUN THE SHOOT ON SET

On-set mode shows the next shot big and clear. Tap Done to move on, Skip to come back to a shot later, and keep your must-have coverage in sight. Daylight mode switches to a high-contrast light theme you can read outdoors, and the screen stays awake while you film.

TEMPLATES FOR REAL SHOOTS

Start from templates for wedding videography, interviews, music videos and short films, or start blank. Everything stays editable.

SHARE WITH YOUR CREW

Export a detailed or compact PDF shot list, made entirely on your phone, and share it with your crew, client or second shooter.

OFFLINE AND PRIVATE

Projects, scenes, shots, notes and reference images stay on your device. No account and no ads. Everything works on location with no signal.

FEATURES

• Shot list planner organized by project and scene
• Guided shot builder: shot size, angle, movement, lens and camera
• Framing previews for every shot, plus your own reference photos
• Must-have and optional coverage tracking
• On-set mode with Done, Skip and daylight mode
• Quick add, swipe to duplicate or delete, drag to reorder
• Templates: wedding, interview, music video, short film and blank
• Detailed and compact PDF export
• Works fully offline

ShotKit is completely free: unlimited projects, scenes and shots, every template, and watermark-free PDFs. No in-app purchases.
