# ShotKit Privacy Policy

Effective date: 15 October 2026

ShotKit is provided by Habibul Hasan ("we", "us", or "our"). This policy explains how the ShotKit Android application handles information.

## Information stored on your device

ShotKit stores projects, scenes, shot details, notes, preferences, and completion status locally on your device. When you choose a reference image, ShotKit imports an app-local copy selected through the Android system picker. We do not operate an account system or a server that receives this production content.

## Purchases

ShotKit includes a 10-day free trial. After the trial, an optional one-time in-app purchase ("Lifetime unlock") keeps creating and editing projects available. Without it, you can still open your projects, use On-set mode, and export PDFs.

Purchases are processed by Google Play under Google's terms and privacy policy. ShotKit does not receive or store your payment details. To know whether you bought the unlock, the app asks Google Play which ShotKit purchases your Google account owns and keeps a note of the result on your device. No purchase information is sent to us.

## Exporting and sharing

When you create or share a PDF, the file is generated on your device. Sharing occurs only when you initiate it and choose another application or destination. The receiving application handles the file under its own terms and privacy policy.

## Data collection, advertising, and analytics

The current version of ShotKit does not include advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, crash-reporting SDKs, user accounts, or developer-operated cloud synchronization. Google Play's billing library is included only to sell and restore the lifetime unlock. We do not sell user data.

## Permissions

Reference images are selected through Android's system photo picker; ShotKit does not request broad access to your media library. The app does not request location, contacts, microphone, or camera permissions.

## Retention and deletion

Local content remains on your device until you delete the relevant item, clear ShotKit's app data, or uninstall ShotKit. Files you intentionally export or share may remain in the destination you selected and must be deleted there separately.

## Children's privacy

ShotKit is a professional production tool and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through a developer-operated service.

## Security

ShotKit minimizes data transfer by keeping production content on-device. No method of storage is completely secure, so protect access to your Android device and any exported files.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when ShotKit's features or legal obligations change. The effective date above will be revised when an update is published.
